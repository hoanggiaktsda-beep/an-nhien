## D03 — Timber supports / ridge (schematic, NOT 1:20 construction detail)

- Timber end at Z ±3.0m; south glass plane around Z +3.1m, so there is no direct overlap with glazing.
- At Z +3.0m the nominal timber bottom is about Y +3.1275m (ceiling reference +3.4425m minus 15mm oak lining minus 300mm timber). This is not a structural bearing detail.

- Ridge connections, member deflections, wind uplift, column placement, foundation and engineering verification remain HOLD.

## D04 — Collision / object classification
- Architectural solids: floor slab, exterior walls, gables, roof buildup and oak lining.
- Glass: transparent visual proxies, NOT structural or certified glazing system.
- Reserved envelope: steel zone only, translucent concept, not steel section.
- Gutter: U-shaped thin-sheet visual proxies and roof flashing, not drained detail.
- Site: bridge 5m and water 24×25m remain unapproved placeholder extents.
- Intentional geometric contacts: roof shell/ceiling datum; flashing and roof edge; wall/floor; bridge/floor; terrace/floor.
- Removed duplicate south architectural header solid to avoid misrepresenting steel reservation as a completed wall assembly.
- Main roof buildup terminates before gutter strip, eliminating its former broad volumetric gutter overlap in the conceptual mesh.
- Full mesh-level Boolean clash testing is **NOT** completed. Coordinate separation alone does not certify clash-free construction.
